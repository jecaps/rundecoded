# Decathlon Book Monitor product facts

This note records product facts transcribed from Decathlon Book Monitor slides
supplied during the catalogue audit on 2026-09-07. It intentionally distinguishes
heel-to-toe drop from stack height and removable spike length.

## Adidas Runblaze

- stack: 33 mm heel / 23 mm forefoot;
- drop: 10 mm;
- weight: 274 g men / 240 g women (reference sizes were not shown);
- construction: Cloudfoam cushioning, mesh upper, rubber outsole.

For catalogue guidance, Kipsonic X-Country uses a derived maximum distance of
12 km based on the confirmed standard 3–12 km cross-country race range. This is
not presented as a manufacturer limit.

The four Kipsonic models have a confirmed 0 mm heel-to-toe drop. The 20 mm
figures are explicitly labelled as stack, and the 6 mm figures describe the
removable spikes. The range slide shows 153 g for Kipsonic Long, while the
detailed product slide shows 158 g at EU 42; the latter was confirmed as the
applicable catalogue value.
